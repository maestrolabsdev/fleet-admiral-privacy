# Fleet Admiral — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Fleet Admiral is a game by Maestro Dev Labs ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles.

## Data we collect
None. Fleet Admiral does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.
The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

## Data stored on your device
Your settings (language, sound) and your current battle and progress are saved only on your device,
so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you
uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## Android Auto
When you play on a car screen through Android Auto, the game runs on your phone. We do not access
your location, vehicle data or any other car information.

## Permissions
The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that handle data (for example Google Play Games achievements or
online play), we will update this policy before releasing them and describe exactly what is used.
