# Fleet Admiral — Delete your online account

This page explains how to delete the online account of Fleet Admiral (Maestro Dev Labs). An online account exists
only if you opened **Online** in the game; battles against the computer keep nothing on any server. What the account
holds is described in the [privacy policy](index.md#online-play).

## In the game
Online → Online account → **Delete account**, then confirm. The account is deleted at once.

## On the web, without the game
1. In the game, Online → Online account shows your **account code** and **account key**. Write them down.
2. Open the deletion form: **<https://fleet-admiral-server-staging.maestrolabs-dev.workers.dev/account/delete>**
3. Enter the account code and the key and confirm. The account is deleted at once.

## If you no longer have the game
Email <maestrolabs.dev@gmail.com> with your nickname (and your friend code if you know it). We delete the account
within 30 days.

## What deletion removes
Your account, its friend list and blocks, your friend requests, your online results (wins and losses), your lobby
entry and your push token, at once. Other players' counts against you keep only a pseudonym that no longer belongs to
any account, and are deleted after 12 months. In games played recently your
nickname is replaced by "deleted"; those games are deleted 24 hours after they end anyway. Reports other players filed
about your account are deleted with it. Accounts not used for 12 months are deleted automatically.

## Other data of the game
- Saves on your phone (settings, battle in progress, career, medals): uninstall the game or clear its storage in
  Android Settings → Apps → Fleet Admiral → Storage.
- Google Play Games data (achievements, scores): held by Google — see
  [Deleting your data](index.md#deleting-your-data) in the privacy policy.
